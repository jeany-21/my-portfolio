Background

LZ Tax is an online service that helps small business owners file taxes with expert advice available throughout the process.

Tax experts will guide customers through the process, from answering questions to final review and filing.
Problem

However, the current communication experience is frustrating.

Customers can't get consistent and continuous support from experts, leading them to consider working with a CPA instead.
Learnings

What users look for in the new experience?

Approach:
  • Aligned with customer and expert teams on technical constraints and existing workflows.
  • Partnered with Expert Ops to understand SLAs and business requirements.
  • Interviewed tax experts to understand their workflows and needs.
01 Build customer confidence that experts understand their situation and provide accurate answers.
02 Make it easy to access relevant context during conversations.
03 Enable seamless, ongoing interactions with experts.
04 Ensure timely responses to avoid long wait times.
Solution

From no-reply emails to back-and-forth conversations

Customers can have a back-and-forth thread to discuss questions with their dedicated expert throughout the journey.
Experts can easily access relevant customer context during conversations, enabling faster, more personalized advice.
